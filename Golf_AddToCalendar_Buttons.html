Three things that bite.

  1. Google wants UTC in a compact dates param. Outlook wants local time with an offset. The formats are not interchangeable.
  2. Line breaks differ: %0A for Google, %0D%0A for Outlook.
  3. September 18 is PDT (UTC-7), not PST (UTC-8). The ITSM webinar in February was PST. Reusing the February offset shifts the event by an hour.
